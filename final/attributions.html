Project Content

Guitar Gear Explorer is an educational website created by Dawon Gregory for WDD231: Web Frontend Development I at BYU-Idaho.

The website's HTML, CSS, JavaScript, layout, and written explanations were developed for this project.

Product Information

Guitar equipment names, specifications, and descriptions are provided for educational and comparison purposes.

Manufacturers referenced include Ibanez, Fender, Gibson, PRS, Roland, Boss, Peavey, MXR, Line 6, HeadRush, MOTU, Focusrite, Universal Audio, and Audient.

Additional guitar equipment information may be found at American Musical Supply .
